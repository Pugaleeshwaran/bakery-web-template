How it started

A kitchen table,
and far too many cakes

What started as birthday cakes for family turned into orders from neighbours, then their colleagues, then people we had never met.

We never set out to run a bakery. We set out to make the kind of cake you remember from childhood — soft, honest, not too sweet, and clearly made by someone who cared. When enough people asked where to buy one, Rosewood Bakery got a name.

The studio is still small on purpose. We bake to order, one batch at a time, because that is the only way to keep the promise on our menu card.

“Taste the freshness… feel the homemade difference.”

The promise printed on every menu card
The journey

How a home kitchen became a bakery

  1. The beginning

    One cake, one birthday

    A chocolate cake baked for a family birthday came out better than expected — and the requests started before the plates were cleared.

  2. Word of mouth

    The neighbours find out

    Orders arrived by phone and by knock on the door. The recipe book grew from two cakes to a proper menu of flavours.

  3. Going small-batch

    Brownies, cookies and bento boxes

    Customers wanted something smaller and shareable. Fudgy brownies, chip-loaded cookies and single-serving mini cakes joined the board.

  4. Today

    Baked to order, every order

    Twenty-four items, custom cakes for every occasion, and the same rule as day one — nothing leaves the bakery unless it is good enough for our own table.

Hygiene first

What “home baked” does not mean

Home baked means small batches and personal care. It never means casual about cleanliness.

A dedicated kitchen

Baking happens in a separate, purpose-set-up space — not squeezed between the day’s cooking.

Sanitised surfaces

Boards, tools and containers are cleaned and sanitised before every single batch, without exception.

Sealed packing

Orders are boxed and tamper-sealed the hour before they reach you, so nothing is handled in between.

From the baker

“If it is not good enough for my own family, it does not go in the box.”

Every order is mixed, baked, frosted and packed by hand here in the bakery. If something is not right, it gets baked again — that is the whole advantage of staying small, and we intend to keep it.
